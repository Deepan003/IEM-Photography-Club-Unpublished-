\section{What's New in Version 1.3}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{newbox}[Version 1.3 --- June 2026]
This release introduces major security hardening, gallery pagination, multi-file uploads, and several admin workflow improvements. All existing features continue to work exactly as before.
\end{newbox}

\subsection{For All Members}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Self-serve password reset} --- You can now reset your own password via a 6-digit OTP sent to your email. No need to contact an admin.
  \item \textbf{Secure logout} --- Signing out now invalidates your session on the server immediately. No one can reuse a stolen token after you sign out.
  \item \textbf{Gallery pagination} --- The Club Gallery now loads 48 photos at a time with a \textbf{Load more photos} button at the bottom. All photos remain accessible no matter how large the gallery grows.
  \item \textbf{Section filtering} --- Filter the Club Gallery by named sections (e.g.\ ``Durga Puja 2025'', ``Street Walk''). Each section fetches its own complete set of photos.
  \item \textbf{Branded 404 page} --- Visiting a non-existent URL now shows a helpful page instead of a blank screen.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{For Admins and Core Members}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Multi-file upload everywhere} --- Upload up to 20 photos at once in the Club Gallery, event galleries, and competition galleries. A thumbnail grid lets you preview and reorder before uploading.
  \item \textbf{Reject deletes the account} --- Rejecting a membership application now permanently deletes the pending account. The email address is immediately free for the applicant to re-apply.
  \item \textbf{Ban invalidates sessions instantly} --- Banning a user now revokes all their active login sessions across every device immediately, not just after token expiry.
  \item \textbf{Delete removes S3 files} --- Permanently deleting a user account now also removes all their uploaded files from cloud storage (profile photo, cover photo, personal gallery).
  \item \textbf{Competition status auto-refresh} --- Competition statuses (Upcoming / Active / Past) now update automatically every hour in the background. Visiting the competitions page no longer triggers a database scan.
  \item \textbf{Complete S3 cleanup on photo delete} --- Deleting a gallery photo now removes both the full-resolution and mobile-optimised versions from storage.
\end{itemize}

\section{Welcome to IEM Photography Club}

\subsection{About the Club}

The IEM Photography Club is the creative heartbeat of the Institute of Engineering \& Management. Founded by students for students, the club is a space where technical precision and artistic vision meet. Whether you are drawn to portraits, street photography, macro, astrophotography, or abstract work, there is a place for you here.

The club organises regular workshops, photo walks, inter-college competitions, and exhibitions. Every semester, members contribute to a curated digital magazine that showcases the finest work from the community.

\section{Getting Started (All Users)}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\subsection{Accessing the Website}

Open any modern web browser and navigate to the club's website. The home page loads with a full-screen hero video, the club's navigation bar at the top, and sections covering competitions, postcards, gallery highlights, activities, and the magazine.

\begin{tipbox}[Tip: Best Experience]
Use Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox on a desktop or laptop for the best experience. The site is fully responsive on mobile, but the Admin and Member dashboards are easier to use on larger screens.
\end{tipbox}

\subsection{Joining the Club}

\begin{steps}
\item Click the \textbf{JOIN} button in the top-right corner of the navigation bar.
\item Fill in your full name, department, enrollment number, roll number, start year, end year, email address, and choose a password (minimum 8 characters). Optionally add your camera and lens details.
\item Submit the form. \textbf{A 6-digit OTP is sent to your email immediately.}
\item Enter the OTP within 15 minutes to verify your email address.
\item Once verified, your application enters \textbf{admin review}. You will receive an approval or rejection email.
\item On approval, log in with your email and password.
\end{steps}

\begin{warnbox}[Important: Approval Required]
Your account is not active until an admin approves it. You cannot log in before approval. If your application is rejected, the account is permanently deleted and you may re-apply with the same email address after addressing the stated reason.
\end{warnbox}

\subsection{Logging In}

\begin{steps}
\item Click the \textbf{JOIN} button and switch to the \textbf{Login} tab.
\item Enter your registered email and password. Click \textbf{Sign In}.
\item You are taken directly to your \textbf{Member Dashboard}.
\end{steps}

\subsection{Forgot Password \newbadge}

You can now reset your password entirely on your own --- no admin required.

\begin{steps}
\item On the login screen, click \textbf{Forgot password?}
\item Enter your registered email address and click \textbf{Send OTP}.
\item Check your inbox for a 6-digit OTP (valid for 15 minutes).
\item Enter the OTP, then type your new password (minimum 8 characters).
\item Click \textbf{Reset Password}. You are returned to the login screen.
\end{steps}

\begin{notebox}[Security Note]
Resetting your password immediately invalidates all existing login sessions across every device. Anyone who was signed in with the old credentials is logged out automatically.
\end{notebox}

\subsection{Logging Out \newbadge}

Click \textbf{Sign Out} in your dashboard sidebar. Your session is invalidated on the server immediately --- your login token cannot be reused on any device after you sign out.

\section{Club Gallery}
\label{sec:gallery}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Club Gallery at \texttt{/gallery} is the curated public showcase of the club's photography.

\subsection{Browsing Photos \newbadge}

Photos load \textbf{48 at a time} in a masonry grid layout. As you view them:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Scroll to the bottom of the page and click \textbf{Load more photos} to fetch the next 48.
  \item The photo count in the top-left updates as more photos load (e.g.\ \textit{48+ photos}, then \textit{96 photos}).
  \item There is no limit to how many photos you can eventually load --- the button keeps appearing until all photos are shown.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Section Filtering \newbadge}

If the admin has organised photos into named sections, a \textbf{Filter} button appears in the action bar.

\begin{steps}
\item Click \textbf{Filter} to open the section panel.
\item Choose a section: \textbf{All} (paginated, all photos), \textbf{General} (photos without a section), or any named section (e.g.\ ``Durga Puja 2025'').
\item When you select a named section or General, all photos in that section load at once --- no pagination is needed since individual sections stay small.
\item Click \textbf{All} to return to the full paginated view.
\end{steps}

\subsection{Photo Lightbox}

Click any photo to open the full-screen lightbox:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Swipe} left/right on mobile or press \textbf{arrow keys} on desktop to navigate between photos.
  \item Photographer attribution and caption are shown at the bottom.
  \item Admin/core/coordinator members see \textbf{Edit} and \textbf{Delete} buttons in the lightbox.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Uploading Photos (Admin / Core / Coordinator) \newbadge}

\begin{steps}
\item Click \textbf{+ Upload} in the top-right action bar.
\item Select up to \textbf{20 photos at once} from your device. A 3-column thumbnail grid previews all selected files.
\item Drag thumbnails within the grid to reorder them before uploading. Click the \textbf{\texttimes} button on any thumbnail to remove it from the selection.
\item Click \textbf{Add more} in the grid to add further photos (up to 20 total).
\item Optionally add a caption, photographer attribution (search registered members or type a name freely), and assign a section.
\item Click \textbf{Upload X Photos}. A progress counter (e.g.\ \textit{Uploading 3/7\ldots}) shows each file being processed. Both a full-resolution and a mobile-optimised version are created automatically.
\end{steps}

\begin{tipbox}[File Requirements]
Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP. Maximum file size: 25\,MB per photo. Files are validated by their actual content --- renaming a non-image file to \texttt{.jpg} will be rejected.
\end{tipbox}

\subsection{Reordering the Gallery (Admin / Core) \newbadge}

\begin{steps}
\item Click \textbf{Edit Grid} in the action bar.
\item If you have not yet loaded all photos, the grid automatically fetches every photo before opening --- no manual action needed.
\item Drag photos within the grid to your preferred order.
\item Click \textbf{Save Order}. The new order is immediately visible to all visitors.
\end{steps}

\section{Member / Photographer Guide}
\label{sec:member}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{rolebox}[MemberColor]{Role: Photographer \rolebadge{MemberColor}{Member}}
Members are the foundation of the club. After your application is approved, you gain access to a personal dashboard where you can manage your profile, submit postcards, follow events and competitions, read club magazines, and track your activities.
\end{rolebox}

\subsection{Dashboard Overview}

After logging in you land on your dashboard. The left sidebar (desktop) or bottom sheet (mobile) shows your available tabs:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{My Profile} --- personal information and activity calendar
  \item \textbf{My Gallery} --- personal photo gallery (photographers and core members)
  \item \textbf{Magazine} --- read published club magazines
  \item \textbf{Postcards} --- submit your postcard photos
  \item \textbf{Events} --- events you are registered for
  \item \textbf{Competitions} --- competitions you have entered
  \item \textbf{Activities} --- workshops and photo walks you have joined
\end{itemize}

\subsection{My Profile Tab}

\subsubsection{View Your Information}
Your name, department, enrollment number, roll number, academic year, and role badge are displayed at the top.

\subsubsection{Edit Profile Details}
Click \textbf{Edit Profile} to update your profile picture, bio (up to 500 characters), and Instagram handle. Changes save automatically.

\subsubsection{Activity Calendar}
A month-by-month calendar shows colour-coded markers for events, competitions, and activities you have joined. Click any date to see the specific item.

\subsubsection{Export Profile}
Download your club profile as a \textbf{PDF} or \textbf{CSV} using the export button on your profile tab.

\subsection{My Gallery Tab}

Photographers and core members have a personal gallery visible on their public profile page.

\begin{steps}
\item Go to \textbf{My Gallery} in your dashboard.
\item Click \textbf{Upload} to add photos from your device (multiple files supported).
\item Drag photos within your gallery to reorder them.
\item Click any photo to open the lightbox. Use the edit or delete button as needed.
\end{steps}

\subsection{Events Tab}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Browse events with status badges: \textbf{Upcoming}, \textbf{Ongoing}, or \textbf{Past}.
  \item Click an event to see its description, announcements, and the event gallery.
  \item If registration is open, click \textbf{Register} to join.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Competitions Tab}

\begin{itemize}
  \item View all competitions and their current status. Statuses update \textbf{automatically every hour} --- no page refresh needed.
  \item Click \textbf{Submit Entry} on an Active competition to open the submission form.
  \item Check the \textbf{Winners} section once a competition concludes.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Postcards Tab}

\begin{steps}
\item Click \textbf{Postcards} in your dashboard.
\item Click \textbf{Upload Postcard} under the appropriate section.
\item Select your image. It appears publicly on the Postcards page immediately.
\end{steps}

\subsection{Magazine Tab}

Select any published edition to read it in a full-page viewer. Swipe or click arrows to turn pages. Magazines are also publicly accessible at \texttt{/magazines}.

\section{Admin Guide}
\label{sec:admin}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{rolebox}[AdminColor]{Role: Admin \rolebadge{AdminColor}{Admin}}
The Admin holds full control over the entire platform. There is typically one system admin account created via the setup script. The admin is responsible for approving new members, granting roles, and maintaining the integrity of the club's digital presence.
\end{rolebox}

\subsection{Managing Member Applications \newbadge}

New members who complete OTP verification appear in the \textbf{Pending} section.

\begin{steps}
\item In the Admin Dashboard, open the \textbf{Members} section.
\item Review each applicant's name, department, enrollment number, and email.
\item Click \textbf{Approve} to activate the account. The member receives a confirmation email and can log in immediately.
\item Click \textbf{Reject} and optionally enter a reason. A rejection email is sent, and the account is \textbf{permanently deleted}. The applicant's email address is immediately free for them to re-apply.
\end{steps}

\begin{warnbox}[Warning: Rejection is Permanent]
Rejecting an application deletes the pending account entirely. Only reject members you have verified do not meet the club's criteria.
\end{warnbox}

\section{Security and Privacy}
\label{sec:security}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\subsection{Session Security \newbadge}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Login tokens are valid for \textbf{7 days}.
  \item \textbf{Signing out} revokes your token server-side immediately. No one can reuse it.
  \item \textbf{Banning a user} invalidates all their active sessions across all devices instantly.
  \item \textbf{Resetting your password} invalidates all existing sessions --- a security measure in case your credentials were compromised.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Upload Safety \newbadge}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Every uploaded file is verified using \textbf{magic byte inspection} --- the server reads the actual binary content of the file, not just the file extension or browser-reported type. A file renamed from \texttt{.exe} to \texttt{.jpg} is rejected before it reaches storage.
  \item File size limits: \textbf{25\,MB} per image, \textbf{100\,MB} per video.
  \item Only authenticated, approved members can upload files.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Storage Cleanup \newbadge}

\begin{itemize}
  \item When a gallery photo is deleted, \textbf{both the full-resolution and mobile-optimised versions} are removed from cloud storage simultaneously --- no orphan files accumulate.
  \item When a user account is permanently deleted, all their cloud-stored files (profile photo, cover photo, every gallery image) are deleted at the same time.
\end{itemize}

\end{itemize}

\section{A Note on Photography and You}

\begin{mdframed}[linecolor=ClubRed,linewidth=1.5pt,leftmargin=0pt,rightmargin=0pt,
  innerleftmargin=14pt,innerrightmargin=14pt,innertopmargin=14pt,innerbottommargin=14pt,
  backgroundcolor=BoxBg]

You joined the IEM Photography Club because something inside you wanted to make images. Maybe you already knew why. Maybe you are still figuring it out. Either way, you made the right decision.

\medskip
Photography is a discipline that rewards patience above talent and consistency above inspiration. The photographer who shows up on a grey Tuesday morning with no grand plan, who shoots anyway, who looks closely at the unremarkable and finds something true in it --- that photographer grows faster than the one who waits for the perfect light.

\medskip
The camera you carry does not define the photographs you make. A phone, a decade-old DSLR, a disposable film camera --- the frame is the frame. What matters is that you keep looking, keep questioning, and keep sharing your perspective with the people around you.

\medskip
This platform exists to make the practical side of being a photographer at IEM as seamless as possible. Use it. Submit your work. Enter competitions without fear of failure --- failure teaches more than watching from the sidelines ever will.

\medskip
\textbf{The best photograph you have ever taken is the next one you are about to make.}

\medskip
\textit{--- IEM Photography Club}
